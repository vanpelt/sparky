Security
Three gates. Each fails closed.
01 · DEVICE
Keychain-stored, auto-rotated.
Short-lived tokens, never on disk in plaintext.
24-hour rotation
instant revoke
02 · IDENTITY
SSO & SCIM from your IdP.
Membership and offboarding follow your directory.
Okta · Azure AD · Google
auto-deprovision
03 · NETWORK
Private by default.
No public ingress to the data plane, or run it all in your own VPC.
private connect
audit log on every read
PERIMETER Pull a token, an SSO group, or the network path, and the next request stops at the door.
